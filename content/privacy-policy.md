# Privacy Policy

At GetFOSS, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit this website, how it may be used, and what choices are available to you.

GetFOSS is an independent publication focused on free and open-source software, Linux, privacy, security, and digital freedom.

## Information We Collect

GetFOSS does not require visitors to create accounts, register, provide an email address, or submit personal information in order to access the website.

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, or other personal information through the website.

## Server and Technical Information

Like most websites, requests to GetFOSS may be processed by the hosting infrastructure used to deliver the website.

Technical information associated with a request may include details such as:

- IP address
- Browser and device information
- Operating system
- Requested pages and resources
- Referring page
- Date and time of the request
- Technical information contained in server logs

This information may be processed by the hosting provider for purposes such as delivering the website, maintaining service reliability, detecting abuse, preventing attacks, and protecting the infrastructure.

GetFOSS does not use this information to build personal profiles of visitors.

## Hosting

GetFOSS is hosted using GitHub Pages, a hosting service provided by GitHub.

When you access this website, GitHub may process technical information necessary to provide, maintain, and secure the hosting service.

GitHub's own policies apply to information processed by GitHub as a service provider.

For more information, please refer to GitHub's privacy documentation.

## Cookies

GetFOSS does not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

The website may use browser storage for basic functionality. For example, the site's light and dark theme preference may be stored locally in your browser so that your preferred appearance can be remembered between visits.

This preference is stored on your device and is not used by GetFOSS to identify you.

## Analytics

GetFOSS does not intentionally use third-party advertising analytics or tracking systems to create profiles of visitors.

If analytics or other measurement technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe their use.

## Advertising

GetFOSS does not currently use advertising networks or personalized advertising.

We do not sell visitor information to advertisers.

## External Websites and Links

Articles and pages on GetFOSS may contain links to external websites, including software projects, GitHub repositories, documentation, download pages, and other resources.

When you follow an external link, you leave GetFOSS and become subject to the privacy practices and policies of the website you visit.

GetFOSS is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of external websites.

## GitHub Links

GetFOSS may link to GitHub repositories and other GitHub pages.

If you visit GitHub, GitHub may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

GitHub is an independent service and is not operated by GetFOSS.

## RSS Feed

GetFOSS provides an RSS feed that allows readers to follow newly published articles using an RSS reader.

Using the RSS feed does not require an account or registration with GetFOSS.

An RSS reader or other third-party application may have its own privacy practices. Those practices are outside the control of GetFOSS.

## Children's Privacy

GetFOSS is a general-interest technology publication and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the website does not require user registration or submission of personal information, there is no specific account system for children or adults.

## Data Sharing and Sale

GetFOSS does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We do not intentionally share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Technical information may nevertheless be processed by infrastructure and service providers when necessary to host, deliver, secure, and maintain the website.

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and its infrastructure secure.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should understand that transmitting information over the internet always carries some degree of risk.

## Your Privacy Choices

Because GetFOSS does not operate user accounts or intentionally collect personal information through the website, there are generally no account settings or personal profiles to manage.

You can control certain browser-level features yourself, including:

- Cookie and site-data permissions
- Browser storage
- Tracking protection
- JavaScript permissions
- RSS subscriptions
- Access to external websites

The exact options available depend on your browser and device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the website, hosting arrangements, technologies, or practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the date at the top of the page will be updated.

## Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, believe that information has been handled incorrectly, or have a privacy-related concern regarding GetFOSS, you can contact the project through GitHub.
